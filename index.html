Case 1: Mr. Jobs

eGFR < 25 mL/min/1.73m²

Patient: Mr. Jobs, 72-year-old male

Height/Weight: 170 cm, 72 kg (BMI ≈ 24.9)

Presentation: Presented to ER with worsening shortness of breath and a painful, non-healing diabetic foot ulcer.

Chief Complaint (CC): “Doctor, I cannot breathe well, my legs are swelling, and my right foot wound hurts badly. Also, I have been taking my Dapagliflozin for nearly 5 years—should I keep taking it?”

History of Present Illness (HPI):

Patient reports progressive shortness of breath for 2 weeks, worse on exertion, associated with orthopnea, paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnea, and bilateral leg swelling. He also developed a right plantar diabetic foot ulcer 10 days ago. Five days prior to ER visit, an outpatient clinic cultured MRSA from the wound and prescribed oral Linezolid and Tramadol/Acetaminophen. Over the past 24 hours, he noted mild restlessness, hand tremors, and decreased urine output. He has been taking Dapagliflozin 10 mg QD for nearly 5 years for T2DM and CKD/HF. Denies chest pain or cough.

Past Medical History (PMH):

  • Hypertension × 20 yrs
  • Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus × 15 yrs (with diabetic foot ulcer)
  • Heart Failure with reduced Ejection Fraction (HFrEF)
  • Chronic Kidney Disease Stage 5 (eGFR 12 mL/min/1.73m², not on dialysis yet)
  • Atrial Fibrillation
  • Dyslipidemia
  • Peptic ulcer disease

Past Surgical History (PSH):

CABG (Coronary artery bypass surgery) 8 years ago

Family/Social History (FH/SH):

  • Father died of MI at 65
  • Mother had diabetes
  • Ex-smoker 30 pack-years (quit 10 yrs ago)
  • Denies alcohol use

Physical Examination (PE):

Vitals: BP 160/90 mmHg, HR 98 irregular, RR 24, Temp 37.6°C, SpO₂ 90% RA

Cardiovascular: Irregularly irregular rhythm, S3 gallop, mild systolic murmur

Respiratory: Bibasilar crackles

Extremities: Pitting edema +2 bilaterally; 2×2 cm erythematous right plantar ulcer with purulent discharge

HEENT: Mild jugular venous distension at 30° elevation, no thyromegaly or lymphadenopathy

Neurological: Mild bilateral hand tremor and hyperreflexia

Laboratory Data / Imaging:

CBC: WBC 11,200 /μL | Hb 10.5 g/dL | Plt 135,000 /μL

BMP: Na 136 mEq/L | K 5.8 mEq/L | BUN 76 mg/dL | Cr 5.5 mg/dL (eGFR 12 mL/min/1.73m²) | Glucose 158 mg/dL

Other Labs: HbA1c 7.8% | INR 1.2 | Lactate 2.4 mmol/L

CXR: Cardiomegaly, pulmonary congestion

EKG: Atrial fibrillation

Current Medications (10 項既有處方):

  1. Tramadol 37.5 mg + Acetaminophen 325 mg/tab 1# PO Q12H
  2. Captopril 25 mg PO QD
  3. Furosemide 40 mg PO QD
  4. Atorvastatin 20 mg PO QHS
  5. Dabigatran 150 mg PO BID
  6. Metformin 500 mg PO BID
  7. Amlodipine 5 mg PO QD
  8. Famotidine 20 mg PO BID
  9. Linezolid 600 mg PO Q12H
  10. Dapagliflozin 10 mg PO QD (已連續服用近 5 年)
